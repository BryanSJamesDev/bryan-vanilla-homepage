About Me

I'm pursuing an MSCS at Northeastern University's Khoury College of Computer Sciences, where I started in September 2025. I build AI agents, search systems and data pipelines, then test what holds up. My B.Tech in Information Technology at Vellore Institute of Technology, India (2021 to 2025), grounded me in data structures and algorithms, operating systems, software engineering, AI, and probability and statistics.

I spent a summer doing hackathons across San Francisco: a robot perception pipeline at the Physical AI Sprint Hackathon, a dynamic-pricing detection agent at a Bright Data hackathon, and something working by the end of each event. Alongside those builds, I completed a take-home patent search engine.

My research work includes the published book chapter "Thyroid Disease Prediction Using ML Models" (January 2025). Below is a honeycomb grid of a few projects I've worked on: hover or tab through a hex to see what it does, then click through to the code.
